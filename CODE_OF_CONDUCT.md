# Code of Conduct

PlayRewire follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We're building a place where people who are new, nervous, or "not technical" feel welcome. Be patient, assume good intent, and remember that every expert was once a beginner.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being welcoming to beginners and answering questions without judgment
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what's best for learners and the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Mocking someone for not knowing something
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information without permission

## Reporting

Report violations to the project owner at **iemafzalhassan@gmail.com**. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The project owner may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban from the project's spaces, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
